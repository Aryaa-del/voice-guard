GOOD QUESTIONS.

A few things
worth knowing.

A little context helps you get more from every result.

Can VoiceGuard detect every AI voice?+

No. Generated voices can be missed, and human speech can be flagged. Treat each result as an estimate, not proof of authenticity.

What makes a good recording?+

Use 5–15 seconds of clear speech with one speaker and little background noise. You can record here or upload a WAV file up to 60 seconds and 20 MB.

What does “inconclusive” mean?+

The recording did not produce a clear, consistent prediction. Try a longer, cleaner clip. An uncertain result does not confirm either a human or AI voice.

Do I need an account?+

No sign-up is needed. Choose a sample, upload speech, or allow microphone access to record directly.
